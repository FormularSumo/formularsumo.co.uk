Hi, I'm James Heppell, a computer scientist from the UK. I'm currently studying a masters degree at Cardiff University (2026-27), and recently graduated in Computer Science and Spanish from the University of Exeter (2022-26), where I spent a year studying abroad in Alcalá de Henares, Community of Madrid. Since 2024 I've been volunteering at [Open Web Advocacy](https://open-web-advocacy.org), helping with regulatory submissions, workshops, website development, and translation.

I spend a lot of time playing music. Mainly sax, trombone, and piano, and I'll happily play in any setting from symphonic orchestra to big band. I also have some [some experience conducting](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IKI8LknMd1M&list=PLxxOvCPuFEb1UJYyw-_zzGBljsXabj3hb&index=9).

I enjoy travelling and learning about people. Languages and cultures, history, geography, and the science behind why we think, act and are how we are; our evolutionary pasts. I like writing about and otherwise sharing what I learn with anyone who's interested.

To round off this brief introduction, I love sports, more or less all of them I've tried. For the past few years calisthenics [street workout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Street_workout) has been my favourite, but other highlights include cycling, swimming, running, and table tennis.
